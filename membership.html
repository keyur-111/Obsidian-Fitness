Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer a yearly plan?

Yes! Our 12-month plan is ₹12,000 — the best value at just ₹1,000/month. Visit the gym or call us to enroll.

Do you offer family discounts?

Yes, we offer a 10% discount for family members enrolling together. Couples and group packages are also available.

Yes, members on 3-month or longer plans can freeze their membership for up to 15 days per cycle.
